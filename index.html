Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, absolutely. SV PLAST is engineered for direct single-coat application over all standard internal masonry surfaces including AAC lightweight blocks, red clay bricks, fly-ash blocks, solid concrete blocks, and RCC ceilings. No sand-cement undercoat or plaster backing is required.

Unlike traditional cement plaster, which hardens through hydraulic hydration requiring continuous daily water spraying for 10 to 14 days, gypsum sets through a natural crystalline process. It chemically locks its optimal water ratio and achieves full structural hardness within 72 hours, saving thousands of liters of potable water per site and preventing efflorescence stains.

Yes. SV PLAST is manufactured with an ultra-fine 200-mesh mineral powder. When applied with a professional steel trowel, it creates a pure white, glass-smooth mirror finish that is 100% paint-ready. Once dried, you apply interior primer and paint directly—saving the cost, labor, and time of 2 coats of wall putty.

Under standard Indian jobsite conditions, one 25kg bag of SV PLAST covers approximately 20 to 22 square feet at an application thickness of 12mm. For smooth AAC block walls requiring only 10mm thickness, coverage can reach up to 24 to 26 square feet per bag.

Gypsum plaster is specifically formulated for internal dry areas (living rooms, bedrooms, hallways, offices, lobbies). For external building facades exposed to continuous monsoon weathering, external cement plaster remains the industry standard. For bathroom wet zones, standard waterproof cement plaster before tiling is advised.
