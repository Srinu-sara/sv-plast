SV PLAST
Ultra-Fine Iranian Gypsum Plaster
Technical Specification Sheet

1. Product Overview

SV PLAST is a premier-grade, ultra-white interior gypsum plaster manufactured from high-purity natural gypsum rock imported directly from certified mines in Iran. Designed for direct application on internal red brick walls, AAC lightweight blocks, concrete blocks, and RCC surfaces without requiring sand, cement, or multi-coat wall putty.

3. Key Quality Processing Steps

Every 25kg bag undergoes state-of-the-art curation:

• Micro-Pulverization: Controlled impact crushing produces uniform micron particles.
• Multi-Deck Sieving: High-frequency vibratory screens remove impurities, ensuring 200-mesh purity.
• Controlled Calcination: Precision temperature conversion preserves hemihydrate crystal strength.
• Hardening Formulation: Delivers superior indentation resistance and impact durability.

4. Application Advantages

• Faster Construction: Ready for paint in 72 hours. Saves up to 70% project plastering time.
• Zero Water Wastage: Completely eliminates 10-14 days of mandatory water curing.
• Direct Single-Coat: Eliminates rough sand-cement plastering + double coat putty leveling.
• Eco-Friendly: 100% recyclable, non-toxic, and drastically reduces carbon footprint.
